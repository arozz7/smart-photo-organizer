# Feat: "Not a Face?" suggestions in Edge Cases (Phase B of the non-face and picture plan). Version 0.8.9

**Branch:** `feature/non-face-suggestions` (from main). Plan: `docs/plans/non-face-and-picture-faces-plan.md`, Phase B (first part: the detector-score list). Measurements: `docs/measurements/non-face-and-picture-faces.md`.

## What it does
A new Edge Cases filter, **Not a Face?** (People > Review Needed row), lists unassigned, not ignored, human faces whose **detector score is below a cut-off**, lowest score first (the most likely non-faces: dark blobs, backs of heads, hair).
**Nothing is ignored automatically.** The user sees every thumbnail with its score, quality and pose and then chooses:
- **Ignore selected** (select page: "Select all loaded"): ignores the faces with `ignore_source = 'non_face_review'`. A banner offers **Undo** for exactly that batch; the faces are also in the Ignored filter.
- **Keep (these are faces):** records the faces in `face_flags` as dismissed, so they are not suggested again. They stay in the review queue.
- **Cut-off** (select on the list, saved in app state as `nonface_cutoff`, 0.30 to 0.75, default **0.55**): every choice shows how many faces it would suggest, and the header says how many of the current list are **turned 45 degrees or more**.

## Profile-safe rules (from the user's label review, 2026-11-19)
Side-profile, turned, looking-down, covered and blurry faces are real faces. So: the list is sorted and filtered by the detector score only (pose, size and quality are never inputs); each tile shows its pose
(an unknown pose reads "pose unknown", never "front") and quality; turned faces are marked amber; the header counts them. In the user's library 819 of the 3,846 faces at 0.55 are turned 45 degrees or more.

## Safety
Every write re-checks, in SQL, that the face is still a suggestion at the saved cut-off (unassigned, not ignored, human, below the cut-off, not kept): a stale page cannot ignore a face that was named, ignored or kept meanwhile.
Undo restores only faces tagged `non_face_review` that are still ignored and unassigned, and clears the tag. A face the user named meanwhile is not touched.

## Measured on the real library (read-only copy)
Same predicate as the list: 55,665 queue faces; below 0.40: 1,170; 0.45: 2,175; 0.50: 3,019; **0.55: 3,846**; 0.60: 4,706; 0.65: 5,992; 0.70: 7,871 (turned 45 or more: 269 / 439 / 622 / **819** / 1,045 / 1,447 / 2,252).
Each count or page query takes 0.24 s. The People page asks for the counts once when it opens.
On the 480 labelled faces (Phase A, corrected by the user) the 0.55 list is 68% non-faces, 7% pictures and 24% real faces (2 of the 10 real ones turned); the lowest scores come first.

## Assumptions and risks
- A wrong bulk ignore hides a real face from the review queue: the user sees the thumbnails first, Undo restores the batch, the Ignored filter restores any other time. Kept faces are not undoable (they just stay in the queue).
- The detector score is the app's own and can change if the detector model changes; the cut-off is then re-chosen from the list's counts.
- Older builds ignore `face_flags` (additive) but show the downgrade warning.
- Not done yet (Phase B part 2): the "is a clear face visible" model question as a second pass, run in the background when idle.
